# Routine: launch

Launch posts on the boards where early adopters look for new products: Show HN, Indie Hackers, DevHunt (developer products) and Product Hunt. You draft each one from the founder's own site, in their voice, and fill the form in their browser. They post it under their own name and stay to answer comments. Every link is tagged, so each launch's visits and signups are counted as its own.

Follow [../references/verification.md](../references/verification.md). Every hard line applies, number 3 most of all: never upvote, never ask anyone to upvote or comment, never trade votes, never post in a group asking for them. Each board removes launches that do.

Input: an optional board (`show_hn`, `indie_hackers`, `devhunt`, `product_hunt`), or `warmup` and a board (`warmup show_hn`, `warmup indie_hackers`) to take part where the board is closed to the founder's account (see "Taking part" below).

## Procedure

1. Call `workspace` with `repo` and `access`.
2. If Show HN could be in play (no board named, or `show_hn`) and `launch_targets` has no `hn` yet, ask the founder for their Hacker News username, once, and store it with `launch_prep` `{ hnUser }` (free): from then on the plan reads its karma every day and offers Show HN again by itself once it opens. Then call `launch_targets` (free). It returns only the boards and steps not recorded yet; never offer one from memory.
3. **Blockers first.** If `readiness.blockers` is not empty, say each in plain words, offer the fix as the move (tracking: finish the install with the fix routine; assets: what the audit says is missing), and stop. A launch is a one-day spike: without tracking it is lost.
4. **Pick the board.** Only the boards that fit this product come back (each with `why`); `notOffered` names the others and why they fit poorly. Never offer one from `notOffered`. If the founder asks for one by name, say its `why` in one line, and only if they still want it call `launch_targets` with `all`. Show the boards left with their `timing`, and recommend one, by who the buyers are:
   - A developer product: DevHunt, and Show HN when it is open to the founder's account.
   - A product for founders and makers: Indie Hackers.
   - Anything else: whichever board the buyers read; Product Hunt last, and only on a day the founder can spend answering comments.
   - **A closed step** (`closed: true`, its target's `gate` says why) is never drafted or filled, and never offered as the launch. Say the `gate.note` in one line and offer, as options: taking part on that board today (recommended: "Taking part" below), another board, or for Show HN the step's `fallback` (a plain Hacker News link post, its `note` in the option's description). The Indie Hackers product page is not closed by the posting gate: it can be filled today.
   - **Show HN** is a story on news.ycombinator.com/submit whose title starts with "Show HN". It is for the product itself, something the founder made and can talk about, that people can try now without a waitlist or a sales call (the `link` points at it). HN says "Don't post quickly-generated one-offs": a free tool your agent built (`readiness.freeTool`) is never the Show HN; it can go up as the step's `fallback`, a plain link post, with the fallback's `link`, recorded as COMMUNITIES, POST without `meta.launch` so the Show HN of the product stays open. Hacker News restricts Show HN for new accounts only, with no karma bar; the way in for a new account is taking part, never a way around it.
   - **Product Hunt** starts with its run-up ("The Product Hunt run-up" below), not the form.
   - One board at a time, a few days to a week apart, so each gets answered and its numbers can be read before the next.
5. **Draft**, from the founder's own site and the product the `workspace` describes. Read the home page and the pricing page first. Plain words, no superlatives, no "revolutionary", no emoji, no exclamation marks (SKILL.md, "Voice"). Numbers only the founder gives you. Keep every rule in the target's `rules`.
   - Show HN: the title (`Show HN: <Name> – <what it does>`, under 80 characters) and the points for the founder's first comment, which HN asks them to write themselves: who they are, why it matters to them and how they built it ("Explain how and why"), what is technically interesting, its honest limits, and the feedback they want. The submit form's text stays empty when there is a URL; the first comment is posted right after.
   - The Hacker News link post (the `fallback`, only when the founder picked it): the step's `fallback.fields`. A plain title with no "Show HN:", no superlatives, and the same first comment. Every Show HN rule holds, voting most of all. Record it with the step's `recordAs`, as the Hacker News launch.
   - Indie Hackers: the product page fields, then the post: the story and the stage it is at, what was built and for whom, a real number or lesson, and one or two concrete questions. One group, the closest to the buyer.
   - DevHunt: the slogan (one technical line), the short description, the pricing type, the screenshots in order (the product itself first), the launch week. Picking the week costs the founder $19 or $49: say so, recommend the free queue, and leave the choice to them.
   - Product Hunt: name, 60-character tagline, 500-character description, up to 3 tags, the thumbnail and the gallery (the product video from the video routine when there is one, as a YouTube link), and the maker's first comment. This is the run-up's schedule step: the founder picks the day and presses schedule.
   Show the draft to the founder and take their edits before you fill anything.
6. **Fill the form** at the step's `url` in the founder's browser, with the target's tagged `link` as the URL. If a login is needed, ask them to sign in. If Hacker News sends the submit page to `/showlim` ("We're temporarily restricting Show HNs"), stop: Show HN is not open to this account yet. Say so, record nothing, and offer taking part, another board, or the step's `fallback` with its `note`. If the Indie Hackers new-post page says "You can't create posts yet", stop the same way: save the approved draft in `.shipfound/launch/indie-hackers-post.md`, call `launch_prep` `{ ihPosting: "closed" }` so it is not offered again until it opens, and offer taking part. When it opens, call `launch_prep` `{ ihPosting: "open" }` and post the saved draft. **Stop before submit**, and tell the founder which button to press and what to paste as the first comment, if the board has one.
7. When the founder says it is up, ask for the link and call `record_action` with the step's `recordAs` (`module`, `kind`, `meta`, always with `meta.launch`: without it the post is not counted as the launch), `title` the post's title, `url` the post or listing page, and for a listing `liveUrl` the product's URL. On Indie Hackers, go straight on to the next step (the post after the product page).
8. **After.** Tell the founder the target's `after` in two lines. For each comment that needs an answer, they can bring the thread URL to the reach routine for a draft they edit and post. Answer critics first and never argue.
9. A week later (grow offers it): `verify` the post (a Hacker News post that was flagged or killed shows as Failed), and read the launch's numbers with `analytics` `{ report: "overview", filters: [{ field: "utm_source", value: "<the target's utmSource>" }] }` (the `conversions` report the same way). Report visits, signups and paying customers, not points or upvotes. Then the hand-over (SKILL.md): offer the next move.

Without browser tools, give the founder the URL and every field to paste, and record nothing until they say it is posted.

## Taking part (a board closed to the founder's account)

Show HN and Indie Hackers posts are held back from accounts that have not taken part. Neither board publishes its bar. Until the board opens, the move is the founder's own comments in threads where they know the subject. Shipfound finds the threads and you draft a comment for each, so the founder is never left facing an empty box. Each draft is notes for them to write in their own words; they post every comment themselves.

1. Call `launch_prep` `{ threads: "show_hn" }` or `{ threads: "indie_hackers" }` (free). With no `topics` stored, it searches by the words the founder's market uses. The first time, ask the founder what they know well enough to answer (their stack, what they built, launches they ran, what they measured), one line, and pass it as `topics`.
2. Open each thread in the founder's Chrome and read the post and the top comments. For Indie Hackers, the search gives no dates: drop any post older than about a week. Skip a thread where the founder has nothing real to add. Up to eight come back so there are spares; a thread whose `topic` is "a live Ask HN" or "a live Show HN" was found to fill the list, not by the founder's topics.
3. Draft at least three and at most five, one comment per thread. If fewer than three are worth it, open the returned `browse` page in the founder's Chrome and pick live threads there until there are three. Each draft keeping every one of the returned `rules`. Head each one "Draft. Write this in your own words before you post." Answer the thread's own question from something the founder did, saw or measured. No product link or name unless someone asked what they use. Two to five sentences. Use only facts the founder gave you or that are in the repo; never invent a number or a story.
4. Show the drafts in the chat, each under its thread link, and leave the reply box empty. For Hacker News, say once, plainly: its guidelines ask for no generated or AI-edited text, so the draft is what to say, and the words are theirs.
5. When the founder says one is up, ask for the comment's own link (its "link" under the timestamp) and `record_action` COMMUNITIES, POST with `title` the thread's title, `url` the comment's link and `meta` `{ type: "warmup", board, thread: the thread's URL }`. A thread recorded or shown is not drafted again.
6. Three to five threads a day at most. Never vote, never ask anyone to vote or comment, never post from another account. `today` offers taking part again the next day while the board is closed. Show HN comes back as the launch on its own once the account's karma reaches the bar. For Indie Hackers, `launch_targets` `ihRecheck` asks for a look at the new-post page once a week.

## The Product Hunt run-up

A Product Hunt launch with no run-up lands on nobody. The run-up is two weeks, built from Product Hunt's own guide: schedule the launch ahead so its page collects followers, a thumbnail and two gallery images or more, the maker's first comment, people who already care told one to one, and never a request for upvotes.

1. If `launch_targets` `productHunt` is null, ask the founder for a launch day at least 14 days out (a weekend day is quieter and easier to place on), and store it with `launch_prep` `{ productHuntDay: "YYYY-MM-DD" }`.
2. `productHunt.steps` holds every step dated from that day, and `productHunt.due` is today's. Show the whole schedule once, one line per step with its date. After that, do the step that is due, as its `detail` says:
   - profile: the founder's personal account, set up in their Chrome; co-makers make their own accounts now.
   - take part: on days with nothing else due, read two or three of today's launches in the founder's space in their Chrome and draft one comment each, the same way as "Taking part" above. Record nothing.
   - assets: the thumbnail and the gallery from the real product screens, and the video from the video routine (a public YouTube link).
   - schedule: draft (step 5 above), fill the form, and stop before schedule; the founder picks the day and presses it. Record the scheduled page with the step's `recordAs` only on launch day, when it is live.
   - tell: one Gmail draft per person who already knows the product, one to one, at most 20 a day, each with the launch page link, asking them to follow it and say what they think on the day. Never ask for an upvote.
   - post: a build in public post about the date (the notes routine).
   - clear, launch, thanks: as their `detail` says. On launch day, record the launch with the step's `recordAs` once it is live, then step 8 above.
3. When the founder has done a step, call `launch_prep` `{ done: "<step id>" }`. `today` offers the next one on its day. A new day passed as `productHuntDay` re-dates the steps left and keeps the ones done.
