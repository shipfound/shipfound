# Routine: boost

A small paid test of which message makes strangers click: on X, the founder's own posts; on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Reddit, ads written for the boost (the Meta and Reddit sections below). They spend their own money in the platform's Ads Manager; you plan it, fill the form in their browser, and read the result. Shipfound never launches, pays or changes a budget.

Input: one or more post links, `meta`, `reddit`, `read`, `stop` or `refresh` (each optionally with a boost id), or nothing.

**More than one boost.** Tests on different platforms can run side by side (a Meta test does not hold back one on Reddit). Pass the boost's id to `boost_results` and `boost_record` whenever more than one is running: without it they take the latest. `read <id>`, `stop <id>` and `refresh <id>` name the boost.

**Which platform.** X, unless the input says `meta` or `reddit`, or the founder asks for Facebook, Instagram or Reddit: then follow that section, which reuses the steps below where it says so. Ask only when more than one is set up and they did not say. Reddit suits a product whose buyers read particular communities (developers, a hobby, a profession); say so when it fits.

## What a boost is

Say this once, in plain words, the first time it comes up: "A boost pays X to show your post to people who do not follow you. The promotion is the test: on a small budget it shows which message makes strangers click. Signups get counted too, but a $50 test brings a handful at most, too few to rank posts on."

- Default: $45 over 3 days, split equally across the posts, each in its own ad group so X cannot hand all the money to one post early.
- **Every boost carries the product video**: its screens recreated in HTML and animated, in their branding, no sound, 30 seconds at most (routines/video.md). A boost shows strangers the product, not only a sentence about it.
- **One cut per ad, and an image of each.** Once the ads are written and approved, and before the plan, run routines/video.md with input `ads`: each ad gets its own cut opening on its own hook (frame 0 is that hook), in 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, plus an image of the ad in 4:5 and 1:1. They land in a folder on the founder's Desktop, `~/Desktop/<product>-ads-<date>/`, named by ad: `ad<n>-video-square.mp4` (X, Reddit), `ad<n>-video-portrait.mp4` (Meta feeds), `ad<n>-video-vertical.mp4` (Reels, Stories), `ad<n>-image-portrait.png` and `ad<n>-image-square.png`. Every file you upload or give the founder comes from that folder, never from the hidden `.shipfound/` folder. Pass each ad's files as its `media` in `boost_plan`.
- Goal **visits** (default): each post runs as a Promoted-only copy, its text plus the product video as a video website card with its own tagged link. Only the ad audience sees it, never the founder's timeline, and every visit and signup traces back to its post.
- Goal **reach**: the post is boosted as it is, for the founder who wants the post itself seen more, so only a post that already carries the video. Measured by engagements; visits cannot be traced to one post.
- A post with few views is untested, not weak. A small account's post can go unseen just because few people follow it.
- **The order is fixed: the ad account, then tracking, then the campaign.** Tracking means X's own pixel on their site, loaded by the Shipfound script already there: they deploy nothing. It records each landing page view, and Shipfound's script fires the signup and payment events when their goals are met. X then optimises for the step the founder confirms, the deepest one the site has enough of (step 5).

## The stranger test

Every message passes this before you offer it, on every platform: a post's new angle, a Meta ad, a Reddit headline. Look from the customer's side: they know nothing about the product, and they do not care.

1. **Write the ladder out loud**, to yourself: the product's line → so what? → so what? → so what?, down to what the reader already feels. "We do growth for you" → "people find your product" → "they sign up" → "the thing you built finally has users, and you did not have to become a marketer." The ad leads with the bottom rung: a pain or an outcome the reader already feels, in their words. The product's name and how it works come second.
2. **Banned in the first line and the headline:** how the product works inside, and its own vocabulary (feature names, statuses, settings, credits, pull requests, "audit", "verify", what kind of tool it is). Also any line that only works if the reader already knows the product ("Your agent ships features" assumes they know which agent).
3. **The headline is a promise, not a targeting statement.** "For founders in Claude Code" fails. "Built it? Now get users." passes.
4. **Each ad in a test is a different angle**, not a reword: the pain ("You built it. Nobody came."), the effortless outcome ("Get users while you keep coding."), the founding offer word for word when one is live. Two ads saying the same thing in other words test nothing.
5. **On Reddit, a person talking**, not a product description: never open on "<Product> is a...".

Check each line against the rules above before you show it; rewrite what fails. The founder still approves or edits every word: the test only changes what you offer first. When the founder writes a line that fails it, say which rule in one sentence, offer a version that passes, and use theirs if they keep it.

## Hard lines here

- Never press Launch, never enter or change payment details, never raise a budget or extend the days without the founder's yes on the new amount. Pausing or stopping is the founder's click too.
- Only the founder's own posts, from their own account. Never boost someone else's post, and never buy followers, likes or views.
- The only post you fill in for their timeline is the one message picked for a boost (step 4, "Not a post yet"): their landing page's own headline and subhead or the line they named, or one new angle you write from what their site says. They approve or edit every word before it is filled in, and they press Post.
- No boost without the product video. If it cannot be made clean, the boost stops with the reason.
- Never press Create event in Events manager, or Create on a Meta business portfolio, Page or dataset, or on a Reddit ad account or pixel: you fill the form, they press it.
- The money is the founder's, not credits: ask for a yes on the amount every time, even when they picked the boost as today's move.

## Procedure

1. Call `workspace` with `repo` and `access`. If the x area is red, they need to sign in to x.com in this browser first; stop until they have. If tracking is not verified, say once that visits and signups from the boost cannot be traced yet and X's own numbers still work, then carry on. If the input is `read` or `stop`, go to step 8 or 9.

   **The video.** Look in their repo for `.shipfound/video/out/video-1x1.mp4` under 30 days old, made with cuts (its `public/video.json` hook passes the stranger test and frame 0 shows it). If there is none, say in one line that every boost carries a short video of their product, its screens recreated and animated, and that it is free, then run routines/video.md and come back here. If that routine stops, the boost stops too: say why.
   **Ad account, then tracking.** Call `ads_setup` (free) and work its `readiness.next` until it says `launch`. Never plan a boost before; `boost_plan` refuses it anyway.
   - `account`: open https://ads.x.com in their browser. An account opens on ads.x.com/manager/<account id>/campaigns: the id is in that URL. The Funding instruments tab, beside Campaigns, shows whether a payment method is on it. Record what you saw: `ads_setup` with `record.account` `{ adAccountId, name, payment }`. Without an account, X asks for a country and a payment method when they start one: they fill those themselves; you never type card details.
   - `tracking`: open Events manager (ads.x.com, Tools, Events manager). The id next to "X Pixel" at the top right is the pixel id. X creates "Landing page views" and "Site visits" itself; they start firing once the pixel is on the site. Check `goals` in `ads_setup`: for a signup goal it can fire on (`firable`), Add event with Event name "Shipfound signup", Event type Sign up; for a firable payment goal, "Shipfound payment", Purchase. Setup method "Define with code" (Shipfound's script fires it), never "Define with URL rules"; attribution windows as they are. They press Create event. Read each event's id from its details in Events manager (ask them to read it to you if you cannot find it), and the "Landing page views" event's id too. Then `ads_setup` with `record.tracking` `{ pixelId, events: { landing_page_view, signup?, payment? } }`. A goal that is not firable (a payment counted from the provider's webhook) cannot fire in the browser: say so in one line and leave it out.
   - **Verify from the outside.** Within a few minutes of recording, Shipfound's script loads the pixel on their site. Open their site in the browser, then Events manager: when "Landing page views" or "Site visits" shows a recent Last recorded, `ads_setup` with `record.tracking.verified` true. If their browser blocks X's script (an ad blocker), Events manager will not show it from that visit: say so, and check again after real traffic. Verified is not needed to plan; done is.
   - If their site already loads X's pixel itself, nothing changes: Shipfound's script fires its events into that one and never loads a second copy.

   **What was boosted before.** Call `boost_results` with `history: true` (free) once, before offering anything. A new boost tests something new: never offer a post from it again, never a new message that says what one of them said, and never the same angle reworded. If the founder asks for one of them by name, say when it was boosted and its verdict in one line, and plan it again only if they still want it (`repeat: true`). When one of them is still running and reads badly, the move is `refresh` ("Refresh", below), not a second boost beside it.
2. **They named a post.** The input is a post link, or they said which one they like. Plan it as it is: no ranking, no numbers, no checks on the post. Go to step 5 with `source` founder. If `boost_plan` refuses it as already boosted, tell the founder when and how it did, and offer a different post or a new angle; `repeat: true` only on their yes. A message they want boosted that is not a post yet (their home page line, a phrase) is posted first: step 4, "Not a post yet".
3. **They asked which to boost.** Call `boost_candidates` (free). Posts boosted before are already left out (listed in `boosted`). From its list keep the posts that are about the problem the product solves and make sense to someone who does not follow them: no inside jokes, no "as I said yesterday", no middle of a thread. Leave out anything X's ad review would reject: politics, health or money claims, before-and-after promises. Read each kept post's `why`; never add a number it does not give.
4. Ask which to boost. Claude Code: AskUserQuestion, multi-select, header "Boost which", the two or three best kept posts as options (the first is your pick, its label ending in "(Recommended)"), each label the post's opening words, each description its `why` in plain words. Codex: list them and ask. If fewer than two fit, offer a new message, posted first: an option labelled "Post a new message, then boost it". Pass each picked post's text along with its link.

   **Not a post yet.** Shipfound plans and scores only existing posts, so a message that is not one is posted first, then boosted like any other. Never offer to boost it as an ad alone: Shipfound could not score it, with no stop rule and no verdict. Post one message. Offer two (three with an offer) to choose from:
   - **The founding offer**, when one is live: call `offer_plan` (free) and use its `live` (the newest offer verified live; `boost_plan` returns the same one as `offer`). First, and recommended, unless a boost in the history already went to the offer's page: then it is the last option, not the pick, and the new angle is recommended instead. One that is only claimed (`offer` without `live`: its PR not merged or not checked yet) is not offered: the ad would land on a page that is not there. The offer as its terms say it, word for word on every number (the price, how many customers, how long it is kept, the money-back days, any end date), plus one line on what the product does. Never round, stretch or add urgency the terms do not have. The ad's landing page is the offer's page: `boost_plan` sets it when no `landingUrl` is passed.
   - **Theirs:** their landing page's headline and subhead as they are, or the line they named.
   - **One new angle**, written by you, clearly different from theirs and from every message in the history, through the stranger test: the bottom rung of the ladder first (the pain or the outcome in the buyer's own words), then the product. Only from what their landing page and product say: no number, customer, result or claim the site does not make, no quote, no hashtags, no emoji. Under 200 characters, the first line able to stand alone.

   Ask which one to post. Claude Code: AskUserQuestion, header "Post", the two messages as options, your pick first with "(Recommended)", each label its opening words and each description the full text; they can edit it after picking. Codex: show both and ask. Only one is posted. Open https://x.com/compose/post in their browser, fill in the text without a link (the ad's website card carries the tagged link), attach the product video (`ad1-video-square.mp4` from the Desktop folder, or `out/video-1x1.mp4` before the cuts are made) with the browser's file upload, and stop: they press Post. Without a file upload, tell them which file to attach. Then take the post's link (read it from their profile, or ask) and plan it as a named post, `source` founder. A post made today has no views yet, which is fine: the boost is the test.
5. **Who sees it.** Ask before planning, in one go. Claude Code: one AskUserQuestion with up to four questions, your suggestion first in each, labelled "(Recommended)"; Codex: the same as one short list.
   - **Where** (header "Where"): the countries their buyers are in. Suggest from the site itself (its language, its currency, the countries it names) and the founder's own country; offer "English-speaking countries" (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia) as an option. Never leave it at whatever Ads Manager pre-fills: that is the founder's own country.
   - **Who** (header "Who"): follower look-alikes of 2 or 3 accounts their buyers follow (propose real accounts from their field and name them), or keywords their buyers post or search (propose 5 to 10 from the problem the product solves), or broad: no narrowing.
   - **Language** (header "Language"): English unless the site is in another language.
   - Gender and age stay any and all ages. Ask only when the product is plainly for one group, and then only that.

   **What it optimises for** (header "Optimise"), asked in the same go for a visits boost: the options are `ads_setup`'s `recommend.options`, the recommended step first labelled "(Recommended)" with `recommend.why` as its description, the others with how many a week the site has. Lean towards the deepest step: a signup or a payment teaches X who becomes a customer, a landing page view only who reaches the page. Never offer a step that is not in the options. **Never optimise past the data**: a step whose option has `enough` false (fewer than about 3 a week) gives the platform nothing to learn from, so it bids high for the few people it guesses might convert. That is how a Meta boost ran at $56 to $62 per 1,000 views, three times its range. Say so in one line when the founder picks it. `boost_plan` refuses it unless `force: true`, and you pass that only after they heard why and still want it. The platform's floor (Reddit's signup) is the exception: it is the least Reddit optimises for, so expect a higher price per view there. Before optimising for a signup or a payment, look at the event in the platform's Events Manager: one listed as inactive, or never received, is never forced.

   Two or more posts for visits: make the cuts ("One cut per ad", above) before filling Ads Manager.

   **The audience matches the message.** The people who see the ad must be the people the words speak to. When the copy speaks to a narrow group (users of one tool, one trade), the targeting is that group alone: no broad interests (Entrepreneurship, Small business, Startup company) beside it, which fill the audience with people who cannot parse the ad. To reach the broad group, write the copy for them instead.

   Then call `boost_plan` with the posts (each with its `media`), `source`, `targeting` as answered, `optimize` as confirmed, and `goal` visits unless they asked for the post itself to be seen. Say each of `brief.warnings` first, and fix what they name before going on. Then say, in five short lines: the spend (total, per post, days), what to expect (`brief.expect`), the stop rule (`brief.stopRule`), who sees it (the targeting line in `brief.setup`), and that they press Launch and can stop it any time. Ask for the money. Claude Code: AskUserQuestion, header "Boost", "Spend $<total> over <days> days (Recommended)", "Change the amount", "Not now". On "Change the amount", ask the number and call `boost_plan` again with it. If the plan says it could not be kept, give them the setup anyway and say the read-out is not available yet.
6. **Fill a new campaign in X Ads Manager** in their browser, from `brief.setup`. Never the Boost button on the post itself: its dialog defaults to $100 a day for 10 days, with no tagged link and no spend cap. If they opened it, ask them to close it.
   - Open https://ads.x.com. Without an ads account, X asks for a country and a payment method: they fill those themselves; you never type card details. Then Create campaign.
   - **Campaign:** named `sf-boost-<id>`. Objective as `brief.setup` says: Website traffic for a visits boost optimised for link clicks; Sales ("Drive purchases, sign-ups, or other conversions on your website") for one optimised for a landing page view, a signup or a payment, with the Shipfound event the plan names as the conversion each ad group optimises for; Reach or Engagements for reach. If the page does not offer what the setup names, stop and tell the founder what it offers instead; never pick something else yourself.
   - **Ad group details**, one ad group per post, named `post <n>`: the daily budget, the total spend cap and the end time from `brief.setup`. The end time is always set: never "Run indefinitely". Demographics: the locations, languages, gender and age from the plan (clear the pre-filled location if it is not one of them). Advanced targeting: the look-alikes, keywords or interests from the plan, and "Optimize targeting" off. Delivery: the optimization goal from `brief.setup` (link clicks for a Website traffic campaign; for Sales, the Shipfound event the plan names, never link clicks), auto bid, placements automatic, pacing standard, frequency automatic.
   - **The ad**, for visits: a new post (not "Use existing post") with the post's text (drop its own t.co link), a headline that is the page's own title, and the post's video as its media (`ad<n>-video-square.mp4` from the Desktop folder), uploaded with the browser's file upload. **Website URL** is the plan's landing page as it is, never the tagged link: no `?utm_` in that field. The tags go in **URL tracking**, which appears under the website URL once it is filled: "Add URL tracking", then Source "x", Medium "paid_social", Name the post's `utmCampaign` from the plan (`sf-boost-<id>-<n>`), Term, Content and custom parameters empty, then Add. Check its URL preview reads the post's tagged `link` from the plan before moving on. Leave "Made with AI" for the founder to decide: say the video was built in code from their real screens, not generated by an AI model. For reach: "Use existing post", only one that carries the video; otherwise run it as visits.
   - **Review and launch:** read the page and check every line of `brief.review` against it: the total, each ad group's spend cap, the end dates, the objective, each ad's website URL (no parameters) and its URL tracking, the targeting. Fix anything that differs before you hand it over, and never hand over a total bigger than the yes they gave.
   - Stop before Launch. Tell them exactly which button to press, then: "Tell me when it is launched." Without browser tools, give the URL, the steps above and each tagged link to paste.
7. Once they say it is launched, call `boost_record` with `id` and `status` running. Tell them grow will offer the read when it is due: after a day to apply the stop rule, and when its days are up.
8. **Read** (`read`, or the due move from grow). Open the campaign in ads.x.com in their browser and read each ad group's spend, impressions and link clicks: link clicks, not "clicks", which counts every tap on the post. For reach, read engagements. Call `boost_record` with those numbers per post (`n`), leaving out any you could not read. Then `boost_results`, and report in this order:
   - **Cost first.** When `costAlarm` is set, say it before anything else: the price per 1,000 views (`cpmUsd`) is far above the platform's range, and that is the setup (what it optimises for, a broad audience, a Page with no picture), not the words. A setup problem is fixed in a new campaign (`refresh`), not by rewriting copy. Read the price per view on day 1: it shows a bad setup long before the click rate can.
   - **Tagging.** When `taggingNote` is set, the platform counted clicks that never reached the site with the ad's tags: the URL parameters are missing or wrong on the ad. Say so, and fix the tagging before judging the message. Show each ad's link clicks and its tagged visits side by side.
   - Then each post's click rate, cost per click, visits and signups, and the verdict in its own words, plus `signupsNote`. The stop line is the platform's own (`brief.stopRule` says it). Act only on the verdict:
   - `too_early`: say how many impressions each still needs. If it names posts to pause, ask, and they press Pause on those ad groups. When the cost alarm is on too, offer `refresh` now rather than letting a costly setup spend the rest of the budget.
   - `stop`: say it plainly: strangers did not click this message, and that is the answer the test was for. They stop the campaign; then step 9, and offer `refresh` with new angles.
   - `winner`: offer two things, the money one first: move what is left of the budget to the winner (a yes on the amount; they change it), and try its opening line as the landing page headline (the test routine, as an A/B test).
   - `no_clear_winner` or `working`: let it finish its budget, then step 9.
   - A boost optimised for link clicks (`optimize` link_clicks, or none on an older boost) while `ads_setup` now offers a deeper step: say once that the next boost can optimise for it. A running campaign keeps its objective; it is a new campaign.
   - Never call a winner the verdict does not call.
9. **Stop.** They stop the campaign in Ads Manager. Read the final numbers as in step 8 and call `boost_record` with them and `status` stopped. Its `then` is the next move: the hand-over (SKILL.md) after they confirm it is stopped.

Close the routine as SKILL.md says, with the money spent in dollars on its own line, apart from credits (boosts cost no credits).

## Refresh

`refresh <id>`, or offered from a read: a running boost reads badly (a low click rate, a cost alarm) and the founder wants to keep testing rather than stop. New ads in a new campaign replace the old ones.

1. **Read it.** The numbers from Ads Manager (`boost_record`), then `boost_results`.
2. **Diagnose in two lines**, one each: **setup** (`costAlarm`: the price per view is far above the range; name the cause it gives) and **creative** (a click rate under the stop line at a normal price per view). Usually it is both, and each gets its line. A tagging problem (`taggingNote`) is fixed first.
3. **New angles.** Write two or three new ads through the stranger test, each a different angle from the others and from every message in the history (`boost_results` with `history`). The founder approves every word.
4. **New cuts and images** for the new ads (routines/video.md, `ads`), into a new Desktop folder.
5. **The fixed setup**: optimise for the step `ads_setup` recommends (no `force`), interests confirmed, no broad ones beside a narrow message, the Page picture set.
6. **Plan it** with `boost_plan` and `refreshOf: <old id>`, so history shows where it came from and the old messages are never offered again. The money: what is left of the old budget (`refresh.remainingUsd`), unless the founder says yes to more.
7. **The founder pauses the old ads first** (their click; on Reddit, from your numbered steps). Then the new campaign is filled and launched as for any boost. Record the old one stopped with its final numbers (`boost_record`, status stopped).

## Meta

The same test on Facebook and Instagram. Meta has no "boost my X post": each ad is written for the boost, a primary text and a headline over the product video, and the test compares those messages. Everything else holds: the hard lines, the video, what was boosted before (a message tested on X is a new test on Meta; one tested on Meta is not), the money asked every time, the stop rule and the verdicts.

1. **Account, then tracking.** `ads_setup` with `platform` meta, and work `readiness.next` until it says `launch`. The account comes in this order:
   - **Business portfolio.** business.facebook.com/latest/settings: the portfolio name is at the top left and its id is business_id in the URL. Without one, they create it (business.facebook.com/overview, Create a business portfolio: name, their name, work email); they press Create. If they have one for another product, ask whether to use it or make one for this product. Record `businessId`.
   - **Facebook Page.** Settings, Accounts, Pages: the ads run under a Page, so it must be the product's own. Not there: Add, then "Add an existing Facebook Page" if the product has one, or "Create a new Facebook Page" (the product's name, category Software or Product/service); they press Create. Record `pageId` and `pageName` (the id is in the Page's settings or URL).
   - **The Page's profile picture.** Every ad shows it beside the Page's name, and a Page with the blank default reads as a fake account: people scroll past and Meta's review looks harder at it. Open the Page as the product and look at its profile picture. When it is the blank default, upload the product's square mark: its app icon or favicon at its largest from their repo (an `apple-touch-icon`, an `icon.svg` or the app icon), or the logo the video kit captured (`.shipfound/video/public/brand/logo.png`) when it is square. Never a wide wordmark (the circle crops it) and never a made-up or AI-made image. An SVG is rendered to a PNG of at least 720x720 first, on its own background with some padding so the circle does not cut it. Click the Page's profile picture, Choose profile picture, Upload photo, with the browser's file upload; the picture is cropped to a circle with a zoom slider, and they press Save. Record `pagePicture` true (false if they would rather not, and say ads will run with the blank picture until it is set).
   - **Ad account.** Settings, Accounts, Ad accounts: one owned by the portfolio (its id, also `act=` in Ads Manager's URL). A personal ad account outside the portfolio cannot get a new dataset: use or add one in the portfolio. Billing & payments shows whether a payment method is on it and what it bills in: record `payment` and `currency` with `adAccountId`. They add the payment method themselves; you never type card details.
   - **Dataset (tracking).** Events Manager (Ads Manager, Events Manager), Connect data, Web, named after the product, then the manual Meta Pixel setup; they press Create. Record its id as `record.tracking.pixelId`. Nothing else: no code to install, no events to create. Shipfound's script loads Meta's pixel on the site and sends PageView, Complete registration (signup goal) and Purchase (payment goal, with the goal's value) as Meta's standard events. A goal that is not `firable` cannot be sent from the browser: say so in one line. If Events Manager asks to set up events with its event setup tool or to verify the domain, say what it asks and leave it: the script sends the events.
   - **Verify from the outside.** Open their site in the browser, then the dataset in Events Manager: when it shows a PageView received recently, `ads_setup` with `record.tracking.verified` true. After a real ad click, an event's details show a click ID (fbc): that is how Meta ties a signup to the ad.
2. **The messages.** Two or three ads to compare, each a different angle, each a primary text (under 300 characters, the first line able to stand alone and the bottom rung of its ladder) and a headline (under 40 characters, a promise). Every one passes the stranger test before it is offered. Offer, in the step 4 way (AskUserQuestion, header "Ads", multi-select): new angles written through the test (the pain, the effortless outcome); their landing page's headline and subhead as they are, only when they pass it; the words of an X post of theirs that did well, when there is one (`boost_results` with history, or `boost_candidates`); one new angle written only from what their site says; the founding offer word for word, when one is live (`offer_plan`). The step 4 rules for a new angle hold: no number, customer, result or claim the site does not make, no quote, no hashtags or emoji. Leave out anything Meta's ad review rejects: personal attributes ("Are you a founder?" addressed to the reader), before-and-after promises, money or health claims. They approve or edit every word. Then the cuts and images for each ad ("One cut per ad", above).
3. **Who sees it.** As step 5, with Meta's options: Where (countries); Who (broad, or 3 to 6 detailed targeting interests from their field, named as Meta names them, matching the message: no broad interests beside a narrow one). **Meta's interests are candidates until you find them**: type each into Detailed targeting's search box in Ads Manager and keep only the ones Meta lists. Meta has no interest for most tools and products (no Claude Code, Codex, Vibe coding or ChatGPT); for developers it has Software development, Web development, Computer programming and GitHub. Plan only the interests that matched; Language; age and gender only when the product is plainly for one group. No follower look-alikes or keywords: those are X's. The same question asks what it optimises for, from `ads_setup`'s `recommend`.
4. **Plan.** `boost_plan` with `platform` meta, `posts` (each `text` and `headline`, no `url`), `targeting`, `optimize` as confirmed. Say the five lines of step 5 and ask for the money. When `ads_setup` recorded a currency other than USD, say the amount in both.
5. **Fill a new campaign in Meta Ads Manager** (adsmanager.facebook.com, the recorded ad account) from `brief.setup`, line by line: the objective and performance goal for the step, Advantage+ campaign budget off and one ad set per ad with its lifetime budget and end date, the audience with Advantage+ audience switched off (the Audience header must read "Advantage+ off" and Detailed targeting must not say "Your suggestion" before you hand over: it cannot be switched after Publish), placements with Advantage+ off, Audience Network unticked under Platforms and "Allow limited spending to excluded placements" unticked (Show more settings then lists the platforms: Audience Network must not be there), and each ad's Page, video, primary text, headline, call to action, website URL and URL parameters. Switch off Advantage+ creative enhancements. Never Boost on a Page post.
   - **The media, from the Desktop folder.** Upload `ad<n>-video-portrait.mp4` as the ad's video (feeds), then in the media's Crop, use Replace on "Vertical" for `ad<n>-video-vertical.mp4`, so Stories and Reels get the 9:16 cut and Meta uses each file where it fits. Then add the image as a second ad in the same ad set, named `ad <n> image`: `ad<n>-image-portrait.png`, the same primary text, headline, website URL and URL parameters. The ad set's numbers stay one angle's.
   - **Uploading a file.** Meta's video upload has no file input you can reach: it makes one on click and opens the system picker. Before clicking Upload, wrap `HTMLInputElement.prototype.click` so a `type=file` input is attached to the page instead of opening the picker, then use the browser's file upload on that input, and remove it afterwards.
   - **Defaults that change the message**, each on by default, each switched off before you hand over: under Single image or video, the format options Carousel and Collection and "Show site link media"; Ad sources, Site links (Meta writes its own link labels, such as "Don't miss out", with urgency the ad does not have); Multi-advertiser ads; Essential enhancements, Add video effects (it comes back on after an ad set is duplicated: check again). Never select an Advantage+ generated image: they invent numbers and claims.
   - **Duplicating an ad set** opens "Apply recommendations" (an extra image, Advantage+ placements) ticked by default: untick it.
   - **Never press Escape in Ads Manager.** In the location search it opens "Publish draft items?" with Publish highlighted. Click outside a field instead.
   - **On the Leads and Sales objectives**, Meta keeps detailed targeting as "Your suggestion" even with Advantage+ audience off ("Detailed targeting inclusions are always suggestions with the campaign settings you've selected"). That is expected there; the Audience header must still read "Advantage+ off". On Traffic, "Your suggestion" must not show. Check every line of `brief.review` against the page and fix what differs; never hand over a total bigger than the yes they gave. If the page does not offer what the setup names, stop and say what it offers. Stop before Publish: they press it. Then `boost_record` with status running.
6. **Read and stop** as steps 8 and 9, in Meta Ads Manager at the ad set level: Amount spent, Impressions and Link clicks, never "Clicks (all)", which counts every tap. An account billing in another currency: convert the spend to USD at today's rate for `boost_record`, and say both amounts. Pausing, stopping and moving budget are their clicks.

## Reddit

The same test on Reddit, for buyers who read particular communities. Like Meta, each ad is written for the boost: on Reddit an ad is a post, its headline the message tested, over the product video. The hard lines, the video, what was boosted before (a message is a new test on each platform), the money asked every time, the stop rule and the verdicts all hold.

**The founder does the Reddit steps themselves.** Your browser tools cannot open ads.reddit.com, so every step there is theirs: you give it as a numbered list in plain words with every value to copy, they do it in their own browser and tell you what it asks for, and you record it. Never ask for card details or a password.

1. **Account, then tracking.** `ads_setup` with `platform` reddit, and work `readiness.next` until it says `launch`.
   - **Ad account and profile.** Give them `steps` from `ads_setup` as a numbered list. Every Reddit ad is a post from a profile, and Reddit will not create a campaign on an ad account with none ("You cannot create a campaign without a profile"), so the steps have them add one (for the product, or their own Reddit account) and give the ad account access to it. From their answer, record `adAccountId`, `payment`, `currency` and `profile` with `record.account`.
   - **Pixel (tracking).** Then `steps` again (now the pixel's): from their answer, record the id (it starts a2_ or t2_) as `record.tracking.pixelId`. Nothing else: no code to install, no events to create. Shipfound's script loads Reddit's pixel on the site and sends PageVisit, SignUp (signup goal) and Purchase (payment goal, with the goal's value) as Reddit's standard events; Reddit keeps its click id in its own cookie, so a signup on a later page still ties to the ad. A goal that is not `firable` cannot be sent from the browser: say so in one line. If Events Manager offers its event setup tool or a conversions API token, leave it: the script sends the events.
   - **Signups first.** On Reddit a boost optimises for signups at the least, never Page visit. When the site has no signup the browser can see (no goal, or only the install's `sf('signup')` guess that has never fired), tracking stays at step 2 until it does: read the signup code and save the goal from what the browser already sees (references/tracking.md, "Signup and payment"); if nothing does, add `sf('signup')` right after a successful signup in their code, as a PR on its own branch, and `goals_save` it. Once the PR is live, sign up once on the site (or ask them to) and have them check Events Manager shows a SignUp.
   - **Verify from the outside.** Open their site in your browser, then ask them to look at the pixel in Events Manager: when they see a PageVisit received in the last hour, `ads_setup` with `record.tracking.verified` true.
2. **The messages.** Two or three headlines to compare, each a different angle, each under 300 characters and plain: Reddit readers skip anything that reads like an ad. Each passes the stranger test and reads like a person talking to the community, never "<Product> is a...". Offer, in the step 4 way (header "Ads", multi-select): new angles written through the test; their landing page's headline as it is, only when it passes; the words of an X post of theirs that did well, when there is one; one new angle written only from what their site says, in the words a reader of those communities would use; the founding offer word for word, when one is live. The step 4 rules for a new angle hold. Leave out anything Reddit's ad policy rejects: personal attributes addressed to the reader, before-and-after promises, money or health claims. They approve or edit every word. Then the cuts and images for each ad ("One cut per ad", above).
3. **Who sees it.** As step 5, with Reddit's options: Where (countries); Communities (header "Communities": 3 to 8 subreddits their buyers read, named as r/<name>, proposed from the problem the product solves; only real ones, open to ads); Interests or keywords if they want them; Language. No follower look-alikes. The same question asks what it optimises for, from `ads_setup`'s `recommend`: signups, or payments when there are enough of them. Never offer Page visit.
4. **Plan.** `boost_plan` with `platform` reddit, `posts` (each `text`, the headline; no `url`), `targeting` (with `communities`), `optimize` as confirmed. Say the five lines of step 5 and ask for the money; when the account bills in another currency, say both amounts.
5. **They set the campaign up** in ads.reddit.com from `brief.setup`, which you give as a numbered list: objective Sales (Reddit has no objective named Conversions), Use a catalog off, campaign budget optimization off, one ad group per ad optimising for Sign up (or Purchase) on the pixel, its lifetime budget and end date, the audience with expansion off, and each ad's video (`ad<n>-video-square.mp4` in the Desktop folder: give the folder's path and the file's name, never a path inside `.shipfound/`), headline, call to action, destination URL and display URL, with comments off. Give each ad's headline and tagged link on its own line, exactly, so they can copy it. **If Reddit rejects the video** (it has, with an internal server error on MP4 and MOV), the step after it says: switch the ad's type to Image and upload `ad<n>-image-square.png` from the same folder, the rest unchanged. A rejected upload never blocks the launch. Then give `brief.review` as the checks to make on the last screen before they launch, and the total they said yes to: never more. If a screen does not offer what a step names, they tell you what it shows and you say what to pick, or stop. They launch and tell you; then `boost_record` with status running.
6. **Read and stop** as steps 8 and 9: ask them to open the campaign in ads.reddit.com and export the ad groups' table (Reports, Export, CSV) and paste it, or read you each ad group's spend, impressions and clicks. A pasted export goes to `boost_record` as `csv`: it maps each ad group named `ad <n>` to its ad (with `usdRate` when the account bills in another currency). Never "all clicks" if the table splits them: clicks on the ad's link. An account billing in another currency: convert the spend to USD at today's rate for `boost_record`, and say both amounts. Pausing, stopping and moving budget are their clicks, from your numbered steps.


## The checklist

Before planning:
- Every ad passes the stranger test: the bottom rung of its ladder first, no product vocabulary in its first line or headline.
- Two or three ads, each a different angle.
- One cut per ad, frame 0 its hook, in 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, plus its image, all in the Desktop folder.

Setup:
- Optimised for the step `ads_setup` recommends; a deeper one only with the founder's yes after hearing why (`force`), and never an event Events Manager lists as inactive.
- Every Meta interest found in the search box; carousel, collection, site links, multi-advertiser ads, video effects and generated images off.
- The audience matches the message: no broad interests unless the copy is written for them.
- The Meta Page's picture set; the Reddit profile recorded.
- Each ad's URL parameters checked on the review screen.

After day 1:
- The price per 1,000 views inside the platform's range? If not, it is a setup fix, not a copy fix.
- The platform's link clicks and the tagged visits roughly match? If not, fix the tagging.
- The click rate once each ad has its impressions; `refresh` rather than let a weak ad spend its budget.
