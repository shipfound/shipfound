# Routine: grow

The daily front door. The founder runs it whenever they sit down to work on growth, without having to know which routine comes next. It looks first, then offers five moves, numbered, with more a number away.

Input: optional. A module ("content", "listings", "fixes", "convert", "offer", "build", "launch", "notes", "research", "ideas") means the founder wants a move from that area today.

## Procedure

1. Call `workspace` with `repo`. Not signed in: run the login routine, then carry on. If `pluginUpdate` is there, pass its line on once. If `repo` is not CONFIRMED, settle it first (SKILL.md, "The repo").
   - `ideasToCheck`: feature ideas from what people ask for, found by reading the founder's site only, so the product may already do them. Before `today`, search the repo for each (the code that would do it, not the words) and call `ideas_check` once with every key: `built` true with the `file` where it lives, and `said` true when the site's own pages already say it plainly; `built` false when the product does not do it. Nothing to tell the founder. An idea the product already does is never offered as a feature (founder, Oct 28).
2. Setup comes first, and grow routes to it rather than repeating it:
   - No audit yet (`access` is null): say the plan is built from the audit, then run the audit routine from its first step.
   - Otherwise carry on; `today` puts the baseline and tracking first by itself while they are missing.
3. If you are in the founder's repo, count this week's commits to the product and read the features shipped the way routines/notes.md, step 1, says (Shipfound's own PRs left out) and pass them as `repoCommits` and `features`, so `today` can offer a build in public note on the week's work or a feature, and the 14-day X Article. Then call `today` (free). It returns:
   - `happened`: facts since the founder's last session (AI visits, AI crawler hits, conversions, what got verified or failed, a new visibility run).
   - `due`: a test that reached a verdict, shipped work old enough to verify (`actionIds`, 1 credit each). Quick things, each with its own short ask.
   - `focus`: what holds the product back this week, read from its numbers, in a sentence or two. The five moves go after it.
   - `offer`: the one move for today (the real work; it is a due item only on a day with nothing else), with `key`, `title`, `why`, `gets` (who it reaches and the first sign to watch for), `then` (what it leads to once its own work is done, like the replies to the people who asked and a post after a feature), `command`, `credits`. Only moves with concrete work behind them: the checks still failing on the live site, the directories not listed yet, the page worth writing with its search volume. Never the same move as yesterday.
   - `after`: the next four, listed 2 to 5. `more`: every other move with work behind it, best first, for a founder who wants to look further. `catalog`: every other kind of move Shipfound makes (a build in public post, a product video, a boost, a question about the numbers...), so the founder can find what exists without knowing to ask; `ready: false` means it needs something first, and its `why` starts with what. `resting`: moves held back because they were declined lately. `held`: moves held back on purpose until an earlier change can be read (a home page change is read on its funnel step 28 days each side), each with `until` and `why`.
   - The week's sets come first, by the founder's rule, until that week's is staged or shipped: for an app, "Your App Store listing this week" (the listing fixes, the name, subtitle and keyword field aimed at the searches it can win in each language, the next language; the aso routine's `set`); for a website, "SEO and AI search this week", which also carries the week's study findings (the idea most people ask for and a move from products a step ahead), each with a link to its tab in `links`.
   - Each move may carry `evidence`: `signal` (a number of the founder's own, like the funnel's biggest leak), `past` (what this kind of move did for this founder before), `asked` (people who asked for it in public), `peers` (products a step ahead that do the same, from the founder's peer study), and `grade` and `library` (the best play of that kind in Shipfound's play library: A is a dated trace before a verified revenue rise). `rests` is that evidence in one line: the founder's own first (or who asked), then the peer study, then the library. Shipfound has already judged the moves for this product, with what the founder said to grow before: which five to offer, the order of the rest, and each move's `why`, the reason to do it today (what the move does is in `does` when `why` is that reason), and `gets`. A kind of move the founder was shown on three days in two weeks and never took leaves the choices while others are open, so the choices change. Keep the order and the reasons as given.
   - `research`, on Builder and Growth when this run started the week's free study of products a step ahead of the founder: say it in one plain line after the brief, as `next` says (it is running, free, and what it finds shows up at the next grow). Nothing to ask; carry on. Once it is in, `happened` says so with the link to its tab, and its best finding is in the week's SEO and AI search set; without that set, its patterns are moves keyed `RESEARCH:...`, at most one among the five.
   - `ideas`, the same way, when this run started the week's free feature ideas study: one plain line after the brief, as `next` says. Once in, `happened` says so with the Ideas tab, and the idea most people ask for is in the week's set; without the set, ideas are moves keyed `IDEA:...`, at most one among the five.
   - `confirm`: what only you can confirm, from the founder's own tools, before the brief. Do it first, free:
     - `confirm.prs`: Claimed PRs that Shipfound cannot see because the repo is private. In the repo, run `gh pr list --repo <repo> --state all --limit 200 --json url,state,mergedAt` for each of `confirm.prs.repos`, and pass every PR whose number is in `confirm.prs.numbers` to `record_merges` in one call. A merged PR is checked at once (its live page, from outside). Put the result in the brief as one `happened` line: "4 merged PRs are now Verified, 1 waits for its page to go live."
     - `confirm.listings`: directories where a listing was submitted but only the submit or account page was recorded. If the audit showed Gmail open, search the founder's Gmail for an approval or "you're live" email from each `directory` since `submittedAt` (read only, never reply). Where one links the listing page, `record_action` it (kind LISTING, url = that page); it replaces the submitted one and the server checks it for the link. No email yet: leave it, it waits.
     - `confirm.reddit`: what Reddit said back since the last read. If the audit showed Gmail open, search the founder's Gmail with its `query` (read only, never reply) and read each email that may be a removal ("u/AutoModerator replied to your comment in r/...", "Your post was removed"), a ban ("permanently banned from participating in r/...") or a ban lifted. Pass them all to `reddit_notices` in one call, each with its Gmail message id, subreddit, kind, date, the reason in a few words and the removed comment's link when the email has one; an empty list when there are none, so the read counts. Leave out replies, upvotes and digests. A removal closes that subreddit to drafts until the founder says to try it again (`reopen`); a ban closes it for good. Put each one first in the brief, one line, as its `line` says. Never suggest posting in a banned subreddit from any account: Reddit treats that as ban evasion.
   If the founder named a module, use the first move of that module among `offer`, `after`, `more` and `catalog`; if there is none, say so in one line and offer `offer`.
4. Print the brief in under 10 lines:
   - First run (`firstRun`): one line on what grow is: "Run this whenever you sit down to work on growth. It checks what changed and what is still failing, then offers five moves."
   - `happened`, one line each, as given, links included. Nothing happened: say "Quiet since <date>."
   - `due`, one line each, with its price: "The fix for your Google snippet went live 2 days ago. Checking it is live is 1 credit." "Test of the pricing headline: the new one wins."
   - `focus`, when it is there: one line, "This week: <focus>".
   - `held`, one line each when there is any: what waits, until when, and why, so the founder sees that not changing something yet is a choice.
   - `credits.ask`, if it is there: one line, as given (what they can buy and the billing link). The founder buys; you never do.
5. Then the moves, as a numbered list in plain text, in Claude Code and Codex alike, never a question with choices (founder, Oct 28). Word each the way "Asking the founder" (SKILL.md) says: plain words, no jargon.
   - 1 is `offer`, then each move in `after`, 2 to 5, in the order `today` gives. Each move: its title and price on one line ("1. SEO and AI search this week: rewrite 3 pages, 1 feature people ask for. Free."), then one short line with the reason from `why` with its number and what it gets from `gets`, "After it: <then>" when it has `then`, and what it rests on from `rests` in a few words when `why` does not already say it (a grade C play is the founder's own account with no outside trace; never call any of it proven). When it has `links`, one more line with each link as given ("Feature ideas: <url>").
   - Then "6. Show me more", with how many other moves there are (`more` and `catalog` together) and the first two of `more` in a few words each.
   - Then one line: reply with a number, or say what you want in your own words; with `due` items, also that "check" runs them first, with their total price.
   - **6, Show me more**: print every other move (`more`, then `catalog`), numbered on from 7, one line each with its reason in a few words and its price, and for one with `ready: false` what it needs first. Then ask for the number of the one they want. Every move Shipfound makes is in `more` or `catalog`, so the founder can reach each one. A boost (a small paid test on X, Meta or Reddit) can be among them: say on its line that it spends their own money, $30 to $100 they approve inside the boost, and that the ad account and tracking come first. Never present it as free.
   - **"check"**, or due items named: do them first (a verify; a closed test through the test routine; a boost read through the boost routine), report each result in one line, then start the move picked.
   Pass the answer to `grow_answer` before anything else: `yes` on the key of the move picked, with `from` "first" (1 to 5) or "all" (7 on); a pick from 7 on also passes `seen`, the keys of moves 1 to 5. "Show me more" and "done for now" record nothing by themselves.
   - **The founder types their own answer** instead of a number: pass their words as they wrote them as `said`, so Shipfound's ranking reads them as their direction from then on. Each move they name is `yes` on its own key with `from` "typed", the first one with `said`, and you do them one after another in their order, each as its own job ("Build Bing index checks and the AI bot audit, then reply to the people who asked, then a build in public post": the two features, then the replies, then the post). A typed no is `no` on the offer's key, with `said`.
   - A move picked (or a yes): start it. A `catalog` move with `ready: false`: say in one line what it needs and offer to set that up first (most need the audit for that area; tracking goes through the fix routine).
     - A routine (`command` is a routine's command, with its arguments): read that routine and run it from its first step, with those arguments. The week's App Store set is the aso routine with `set`; the week's SEO set is the seo routine, which does the study findings too (routines/seo.md, step 13). The price rule applies as usual (over 5 credits: the price and the balance, then wait for a yes; this yes counts when it named the price).
     - A move from the study of products a step ahead (its key starts with `RESEARCH:`) routes the same way, through the routine its `command` names; its `why` is what those products do. Every `record_action` for it carries `meta.research` with the move's key, so it is never offered again (routines/research.md, step 6).
     - A feature idea (its key starts with `IDEA:`) runs the ideas routine with that key as its input; its `why` is who asks and where. Its record carries `meta.idea` (routines/ideas.md, steps 6 to 8).
     - A play for one step of the funnel (its key starts with `PLAY:`) runs the convert routine with `play:<id>` as its input: an A/B test of the change against today's page (never a change to just use, however few visitors), on which page, with that step's own numbers and what the play rests on (the play library's companies with their grade, or a common practice with nothing measured behind it; say which). Its record carries `meta.plays` with the id, so it is never offered again. A home page change being read holds only home page plays, not those for the signup page, pricing or the first screen after signup.
     - `verify`: call `verify` once for each id in `actionIds`, then report each state. PRs in a private repo were already confirmed through `confirm.prs` and `record_merges`; one still open is left out, no credit spent. Never say done for anything that is not VERIFIED.
     - `visibility_run`: call it (15 credits, the yes named the price) and say where the result will show.
   - A no to every one of the five: say what is `resting`, and that 6 shows every other move.
   - Done for now: stop, and name what is `resting` if anything is.
6. When the move is finished (its last step done, including anything the founder does by hand right then, such as posting or pressing submit; see "Only once the current job is finished" in SKILL.md), the hand-over (SKILL.md) takes over: `record_action`'s `next` is the next move. Stop when the founder says they are done, and tell them to run grow next time they sit down, the way their host runs it (the command in Claude Code, "What should I do today?" in Codex).

`offer` is null: say what happened, that there is nothing new to do today, and name what is resting. Do not invent work. Then list `catalog` the way "Show me more" does, so the founder can still pick anything Shipfound makes.

Start one move at a time, and never start one the founder did not pick.
