# Routine: video

A short video of the product for a boost on X: its screens recreated in HTML and animated, in the founder's own branding, with no sound. You build it in code on their machine; nothing is recorded, screenshotted into the video or generated by an AI model. Free: nothing runs on Shipfound's servers.

Input: `redo`, or nothing. A boost (routines/boost.md) starts this routine when it has no video.

## What it is

- **Recreated, then animated.** Each scene is one screen of the product rebuilt in HTML and CSS from its own source: the same layout, labels, copy and states. Then it moves the way the product does: what the user does (typing, a click) and what the product does in answer (results coming in, a number counting up, a pull request opening), with the camera moving in on the part that matters. Built with Remotion from the kit in the plugin's [video/](../../../video/) folder.
- **The shape:** the hook (2 seconds), 2 to 5 scenes, the end card (2 seconds). 15 to 20 seconds is right; 30 is the most. Two files: 1:1 at 1200x1200 for the feed and 16:9 at 1920x1080, H.264.
- **Their brand.** The capture reads the landing page: the logo as its header shows it, the font files its headings and body text use, and its background, text, muted and accent colors. The hook and end card carry the logo; captions use their heading font.
- **Only the product, no sound.** No music, voiceover or sound effects: the files have no audio track. Never a screen recording, never the founder's own clip, never AI video or images, no stock footage, no AI people.

## Hard lines here

- **True to the product.** Recreate only screens and steps the product really has, with its own labels and wording. Simplify (drop a sidebar, shorten a list) but never add a feature, a button or a result it does not have. Every number on screen is demo data, plausible for the product, never presented as a customer's result.
- **No one's real data.** Demo names, emails and sites only (`demo-shop.example`, "Acme"), never a customer, a real person, a key or a token, even if the reference shot shows one.
- Never type the founder's password. If the reference shots need the live app, they sign in by hand in the window the capture opens.
- Every word in a caption, the hook and the end card comes from the post being boosted or the landing page. A caption names what the scene shows.
- The video stays in their repo folder until they attach it to a post or an ad themselves.

## Procedure

1. **Is there one already?** In the root of their repo, look for `.shipfound/video/out/video-1x1.mp4`. If it is there and under 30 days old, and the input is not `redo`, show them `out/sheet.png` and ask whether to use it or make a new one. Using it: done, go back to the caller.
2. **Set up the kit**, in `.shipfound/video/` at the root of their repo: copy the plugin's [video/](../../../video/) folder there (if the skill was copied on its own, fetch it from https://github.com/shipfound/shipfound/tree/main/video). Add `.shipfound/` to `.git/info/exclude`, so it is never committed and their `.gitignore` is not touched. Then `npm install` and `npx playwright install chromium` in that folder (a few hundred MB, once). If installing is blocked (no network, a sandbox), say what to run and stop.
3. **Pick the story.** The one thing the product does for a buyer, in 2 to 5 scenes: the input, the work, the output, what it gets them. Take it from the landing page and the post being boosted. Say it to the founder in one line per scene and let them change it.
4. **Read the real thing** for each scene, as many of these as you have:
   - **The source in their repo:** the components, pages and styles that render the screen (its markup, class names, colors, spacing) and the exact copy. For a CLI or an agent, the code that prints its output, so the text on screen is the text it prints.
   - **Reference shots:** write `shots.json` (see `shots.example.json`) with a shot per screen and run `node capture.mjs`. It also reads the brand. It saves `ref/<name>.png` and the screen's text in `ref/<name>.txt`; look at each. For the live app, add `login`: the founder signs in by hand in the window that opens; ask them to use a demo account if they have one. With no screens to shoot (a CLI), keep `shots` empty: it reads the brand only.
   - Their docs or README, for the real commands and flows.
5. **Check the brand** before building on it. `public/brand/logo.png` must be their whole logo and nothing else (not a menu icon, not cut off); if not, set `brand.logo` in shots.json to the right selector and run it again. `capture.mjs` prints the fonts and colors it found; a font "not found" falls back to Inter, so say so.
6. **Build each scene** in `src/scenes/<id>.tsx` and register it in `src/scenes/index.ts` (start from `browser-example.tsx` or `terminal-example.tsx`, then delete the examples). The stage is 1160x900; `BrowserWindow` or `TerminalWindow` frames it. Recreate the screen to match the reference: layout, labels, colors (the app's own, from its source), type. Animate it with the kit (`src/kit.tsx`): `Typing` for what the user enters, `Cursor` with `clicks` for what they press, `Appear` for what the product returns, `CountUp` for a number, `Highlight` and `Camera` for the part that matters. Phone-sized rules: text at least 30px on the stage (34px is the terminal default), at most 6 lines or rows in view, one idea per scene, everything moving or done by half the scene.
7. **Write `public/video.json`** (see `video.example.json`): `hook` is the post's first line or the landing page headline, 8 words or fewer; `scenes` in order, each with its `id`, `seconds` (3 to 6) and a caption of 7 words or fewer; the end card is one short line from the landing page and the bare domain. Leave out `colors`: they come from the brand.
8. **Check the cut.** Run `npm run sheet` and look at `out/sheet.png`: a frame every half second with the safe zone drawn in red. Then render a few frames at full size, `npx remotion still Square out/f.png --frame=<n>`, one per scene at its busiest moment, and put each next to its reference. Fix and check again if:
   - a scene does not match the real screen: a wrong label, a missing part, a color or font that is off
   - text is cut off (a zoom that crops a line someone is reading), overlaps, or is too small to read on a phone
   - a caption is outside the red box or covers what matters
   - a frame is blank, or something pops in with no motion, or the cursor clicks on nothing
   - anything shows a real person, customer, email, key or token
   - it runs over 30 seconds

   At most 2 rounds of fixes; if it is still not right, say exactly what is wrong, show nothing, and stop.
9. **Render.** `npm run render`. Show them `out/sheet.png` and give the two paths: `out/video-1x1.mp4` for the post and the ad, `out/video-16x9.mp4` for anywhere else. Ask if they want a scene or caption changed; a change is steps 6 to 9 again.
10. If a boost started this routine, go back to it. Otherwise close as SKILL.md says, with 0 credits.
