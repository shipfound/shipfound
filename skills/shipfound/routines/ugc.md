# Routine: ugc

An AI presenter video, for an ad or a post on the founder's own TikTok and Instagram. Shot 1: a presenter (AI-generated, an adult who is nobody real) says the hook to the camera as a selfie, about 4 seconds. Shot 2: a hard cut to the same person over the shoulder at a laptop (a website) or holding a phone (an app), the camera still, while the same voice keeps talking. The device's screen is made solid green, and the video kit on the founder's machine puts the product's real screens on it, then the end card over the last line. Made at Higgsfield, priced from its cost plus Shipfound's margin.

Input: `ad`, `post`, `read`, or nothing. Grow offers it as one move (`UGC:week`) whenever there is a brief to write it from, led by the ad while a boost is being prepared or its ads stopped bringing clicks.

## What it is

- **15 seconds at most, 720p, 9:16.** The hook takes the first 4 seconds (11 words at most); the rest is 28 words at most. `ugc_plan` refuses longer.
- **The presenter is AI, and says so.** An ordinary adult who fits the buyers' world, never a name, a celebrity or a lookalike, never a child. The founder chooses once: keep one presenter for this product (the same face in every video) or a new one each time.
- **The screens are real.** Shot 2's screen is the product's own screens, built by the kit's Screen composition (routines/video.md, "The screen") from its source, with demo data. Never screens the product does not have, never anyone's real data.
- **Priced, asked first.** The price is Higgsfield's estimate for this video plus the margin, charged only for what is made: a request Higgsfield fails or flags is not charged. It is over $1, so the founder says yes before anything is made.
- **On the Desktop.** `~/Desktop/<product>-ads-<date>/`, one file per cut, never only in the hidden `.shipfound/` folder.

## Hard lines here

- **Never a customer.** The presenter does not exist, so they never say they used, tried, bought or found the product, never "my results", "my sales", "my kids love it", never a before and after of their own, never "POV: I found this app" (FTC 16 CFR 465 bans testimonials from people who do not exist). They speak to the viewer about what the product does for them. `ugc_plan` refuses those lines.
- **Labelled.** An "AI-generated presenter" label is on screen for the whole clip (the kit adds it). On TikTok, the AI-generated content switch is turned on, for an ad and for a post. On Meta, the AI info label stays on when it is offered.
- **Never the same cut twice.** Each post and each ad gets its own cut: other screens, another hook or end line. Never the same file on TikTok and Instagram, never a repost: Instagram shows unoriginal reposts to fewer people.
- **One account per platform, the founder's own.** Never a second account, an account farm, warmed-up accounts, bought views or follows.
- **The founder posts and launches everything.** You make the files and fill in what they ask; they press Post, Publish and Launch.
- **Never an App Store preview.** Apple requires a preview captured from the app itself (the aso routine's preview part). This video never goes there, nor on the App Store page.
- A Kids category app speaks to the parent through an adult presenter: never a child in the shot, never a line that asks a child to download or buy.

## Procedure

1. **Two questions, asked once.** Call `ugc_plan` with no script. When it answers `ASK`, ask what is open, in one go. Claude Code: AskUserQuestion with "Paid or organic?" (Paid: an ad in a boost / Organic: a post on your own account), "Which platforms?" (Meta, that is Instagram and Facebook / TikTok / Both) and, the first time, "The same presenter every time?" (Keep one for this product: the same face in every video / A new one each time). Codex: the same three as one short numbered question. Input `ad` or `post` answers the first. Pass the answers to `ugc_plan`; they are kept, so a later video asks only what is open. When the founder says otherwise ("organic this time", "TikTok only"), pass the new answer.
2. **The accounts, before anything is priced.** `ugc_plan` says for each platform whether it is ready. Nothing is priced or made until each is.
   - **Paid:** the ad account and the pixel. Meta: the boost routine's Meta setup (`ads_setup`): the founder creates the ad account and adds the payment method, Shipfound's script loads the pixel. TikTok: the boost routine's TikTok setup when it has one; otherwise in the browser, TikTok Ads Manager: the founder creates the account and adds the payment method, then the TikTok pixel. Then come back here.
   - **Organic:** Instagram (a Business or Creator account, linked to Meta) and TikTok (a Business or Creator account, a link in the bio with its tag). What is not ready: the social routine's setup, for just those platforms, then come back here.
   - When it answers `CHECK` (the audit has no social area yet, or Shipfound cannot read that ad platform): open each in the browser and look. Ready: call `ugc_plan` with `checked` true. Missing, or no link in the bio: say what in one line and stop. Nothing was spent.
3. **The presenter** (a new one, or none kept yet): who they are in one line (age, look, clothes), where they sit (in the buyer's world: a home office, a kitchen table, a shop counter), man, woman or person, and the accent of their English. Never a name or anyone real.
4. **The script.** From the customer's side: the reader knows nothing about the product and does not care. Ladder the product's line with "so what?" three times to what they feel (routines/video.md, "The words: the stranger test"). The hook is the bottom rung, said to the camera. Shot 2 is written as lines, each about one screen the viewer sees as it is said: what they get, on that screen. Number the screens 0, 1, 2 in the order they play (the beats of video.json's `screen`, step 9); each line names its beat, or several in order, and every beat is named once. The last line is the next step, said under the end card: the link in the bio, or for an app its name on the App Store; for an ad, the button. For an ad, the ad's own angle from the boost: one angle per video. Show the founder the hook, each line with the screen under it, and the end line, and wait for their yes; change what they say.
5. **The price.** Call `ugc_plan` with `hook`, `lines` (each `{ text, beat }`), `end` and, for a new presenter, `who`. `FIX`: rewrite what it names and call again. `UPLOAD`: the saved portrait is gone from Higgsfield; upload `.shipfound/ugc/presenter.png` with curl exactly as it says, then call again with `portraitUploaded` true. `PRICED`: write its `ugcJson` to the kit's `public/ugc.json` as it is (`beats` is how many screen beats it names), then say the price in one line (Higgsfield's cost plus Shipfound's margin, charged only for what is made) and the balance, and wait for a yes. No yes, nothing is made.
6. **Make it.** `ugc_start` with the job. Call `ugc_status` about every 30 seconds for the images, every minute or two for the clip, with one short line while it runs.
7. **Check the images** (`frames`). Download both to `.shipfound/ugc/<job>/` and look at each: the same person in both, an adult who looks like nobody real; the screen solid flat green, all four corners in view with nothing over them, facing the person; no text, no logos. A new presenter: show the founder the portrait. Good: `ugc_start` with `frames` ok. Not good: `frames` stop (charged for the images only), say why in one line, and offer one new plan with the fix. Never a loop.
8. **The clip** (`done`). Download `video` to `.shipfound/ugc/<job>/clip.mp4` at once: Higgsfield deletes it after 7 days. A presenter the founder keeps: also `portrait` to `.shipfound/ugc/presenter.png`, which later videos upload again. `failed`: say its note in one line; nothing was charged for that step. One rewrite at most.
9. **The screens on the green.** The kit is `.shipfound/video/` at the root of their repo; set it up as routines/video.md says (steps 2 to 5) if it is not there.
   1. `public/ugc.json` is the `ugcJson` from step 5: the hook, a line per beat, the end line marked `end`, the device and the label. Write it again from `ugc_status`'s `ugcJson` if it is not there.
   2. `public/video.json`'s `screen` has exactly the beats the lines name, in that order (routines/video.md, "The screen"): beat 0 is the screen line 1 is about, and so on. Build or change the beats from the product's real screens; ugc.mjs refuses a beat no line names.
   3. `npm run ugc -- --clip .shipfound/ugc/<job>/clip.mp4` from the kit folder (add `--desktop` only for another folder). It finds the cut and the time of each line, retimes and renders the Screen, fits it to the green's four corners, puts the label on every presenter shot and the end card under the last line, and writes `ugc-video-vertical.mp4` (9:16) and `ugc-video-portrait.mp4` (4:5) with a contact sheet of each to `out/ugc/`, copied to `~/Desktop/<product>-ads-<date>/`. When it stops, it says why (a line's `at` to pin it, a beat no line names, a screen that moves too much): fix the script, the screen or the clip, and run it again.
10. **Check the sheets.** Open `ugc-sheet-vertical.png` and `ugc-sheet-portrait.png` in `out/ugc/` and look at every cell: the screen sits on the device with no green edge and can be read, the label is on every presenter shot, each beat changes as its words are said, the end card is under the last line, the lips match the voice, nothing is cut off in 4:5, and the top and bottom 12% have no text (TikTok and Reels draw over them). Fix and run step 9.3 again until each passes.
11. **Save.** The videos and sheets are in `~/Desktop/<product>-ads-<date>/`; check they are there, or copy them from `out/ugc/`. Both platforms: a second cut for the second one (other screens or another end line, its own `ugc.json`, rendered with `--out out/ugc-2`), never the same file on both. Then save each video to the Assets tab with `assets_save`, when this version has it (not built yet in every version: when it is missing, say in one line the files are on the Desktop only). Show the folder and list its files.
12. **Hand over.**
    - **Paid:** back to the boost routine for that platform, with these cuts as the ads' video in place of the kit's own cuts (each ad's `media` in `boost_plan`), one cut per ad. In the ad, TikTok's AI-generated content disclosure is on.
    - **Organic:** the founder posts from their own account. TikTok, from the phone app (AirDrop the cut to the phone; never the web uploader): the cut, a caption (one line in their voice ending "Link in bio", never a URL, then 3 to 5 hashtags their buyers use), a sound from the Commercial Music Library, and under More options, AI-generated content on and Content disclosure, Your brand on (routines/social.md). Instagram: the other cut as a Reel, its own caption, the AI info label on. The bio link is `links` from `ugc_status` (utm tags for a site, an App Store campaign for an app): the founder sets it on that profile while this is the newest video. When they give the post's link, `record_action` with `module` COMMUNITIES, `kind` POST, `url` the link, `meta` `{ type: "ugc", platform: "tiktok" | "instagram", job, cut, campaign }`, then `verify` (TikTok is read by its public embed; Instagram stays Claimed). Grow leaves the AI video until Monday once one is made.
13. **Promote** (organic, once the post's link is recorded). Ask once per post: Claude Code: AskUserQuestion "Promote this post?" (Yes: with the app's own Promote or Boost, your money, you set the budget and press Pay / Not now); Codex: the same as one short question. Not now: go on. Yes, for each platform it was posted on:
    - **Instagram** (Boost post, on the post or in the browser): goal visits to the website with the bio link from `ugc_status`'s `links` whole, never "Visit Instagram profile"; audience Create your own (the brief's countries and 3 to 6 interests that match the hook), never "People similar to your followers" while the account has few followers; Enhancements (Meta's AI edits) off. You can fill these in the founder's browser; they set the budget and duration and press Continue to payment and Pay.
    - **TikTok** (Promote video): the founder opens it in the TikTok app or Studio, picks the website-visits goal with the same link when it is offered (else Reach more people), sets the budget and presses Pay. Tell them, in these words or close: "If TikTok rejects it or says the post is not eligible for the For You feed, tell me and I'll make a video from your product's screens instead." On that word: say in one line that TikTok holds back this AI video, keep it with `grow_notes_save` ("TikTok holds back AI presenter videos: TikTok gets product videos, AI cuts go to Instagram and ads"), and offer the product video (routines/video.md, free) as the next TikTok post, posted and promoted the same way. Never a new AI cut for TikTok to try again while that note holds.
    - Keep what was promoted with `grow_notes_save`, one line: the platform, the post's link, the budget and the days the founder gives. The read (step 14) says it beside that post's results.
14. **Read** (input `read`, or at the next video). For a site, `analytics` with `utm_campaign` `sf-ugc-<id>`: its visits and signups count to the post after 14 days. For an app, `app_downloads`' `byCampaign` (Apple leaves out a campaign under 5 people). Ask the founder for each post's views from the app's own insights. Say per video: views, visits or downloads, signups, and which hook to make again.
