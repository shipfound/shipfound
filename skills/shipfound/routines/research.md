# Routine: research

A study of up to 8 products a step ahead of the founder, to learn how they grew. Shipfound finds them through three lenses (`peers[].lens`): **same job** (it does the founder's job in any form, found through AI answers, alternatives to the product, Product Hunt and Show HN; three at most, and `peers[].job` says whether it does the whole job or one piece of it, at most one piece in a study), **same buyers** (the founder's buyers also pay for it, for another job) and **sells the same way** (installed, sold or priced the way the founder's product is). It reads what each shows in public today (the home page and the way to signup, free tools, pricing, launches, listings, their best X posts) and how it started (`read.early`: its home page in the Wayback Machine's first captures and as it grew, its launches with points, its early posts), and the founder's site the same way. The founder may also name products they have in mind: those are studied first, in this study and every one after it (`named` lists the ones kept, at most 5), and count towards the 8.

Each pattern is `when` "today" (two or more of them do it and this site does not) or "early" (a move they made when they started; one product is enough when it has outside proof, a launch with real points or its archived page). Each has a first `step` for the founder's own product, and is carried out by the routine that does that kind of work: a free tool by build, the home page, a trial or a free plan by convert, a launch by launch, a post by notes; a launch price for the first customers by offer; a pricing model or an enterprise tier is the founder's decision, this routine.

**For an iOS app** (`product.kind` "app") the products are apps, read on the App Store: up to 8 apps that come up in the top results of the app's own App Store searches (the ones the week's listing set checks, in each country it checks) with more ratings than it, the closest first; the founder may name apps too, by their App Store links. For each one, `peers[].app` is its listing (name, subtitle, price, in-app purchases with their prices, rating and how many ratings, screenshots, when it last shipped and the versions its reviews were written on) and its latest public reviews, counted by what they say about the trial, the price, the subscription, the paywall and ads. `peers[].domain` is the app's key (`id<App Store id>.apps.apple.com`), `peers[].why` the searches it tops and its ratings against the app's, and `founder.app` the app's own listing read the same way. Each pattern is what two or more of those apps do today (no early moves), and is carried out by a part of the aso routine: their name and subtitle words by the aso routine's week's set (step 27), their screenshots by a product page test (`test`), their paywall and trial by `trial`, their in-app purchase names by `purchases`, their prices by `prices`, their ratings by `rating`, their updates by `featuring`. It needs the app's App Store search check first, which the week's listing set runs.

It is a study to learn from, and it stands on its own: it never says what another product changed, and it is not run to keep an eye on anyone. Call them "products a step ahead of you" ("apps a step ahead of you" for an app), by name.

Input: a move key from grow (`RESEARCH:<kind>:<slug>`), `run` for a new study, or nothing.

## Who pays

- Every study is paid from the balance: `research_run`, $0.88, charged only when 2 or more products could be read. Grow never starts one by itself; state the price and the balance and wait for the founder's yes first, every time. At most one study a day.

## Hard lines here

- **Learn, never copy.** A move is the founder's own version of what the products do, in their own words and design. Never another product's words, name, screenshots or layout on the founder's site, and never another app's name or a trademark in the founder's App Store listing. Their quotes, and their reviewers' words, are evidence for the founder, nothing more.
- **Public pages only.** You never sign up for, email, message or post about a product studied.
- **Never about change.** Never say what a product added, dropped or changed, and never compare one study with the one before.

## Procedure

1. Call `workspace` with `repo`. If there is no `research` tool, say in one line that studies of products a step ahead are not open yet, and stop. An app (`product.kind` "app") is studied on the App Store, as above.
2. Call `research` (free). It returns `study` (the latest finished one, or null), `running`, `nextFreeAt`, `price`, `results` (the page in the results app), `lastAttempt` when the last try did not finish, `skipped` (products the founder removed) and `patternsDone` (patterns already done, left out).
   - `running`: say a study is running and what it finds comes in a few minutes, and stop. Never start another.
   - `lastAttempt`: say its note in one line.
   - No move key in the input (the founder started this routine on its own, or said `run`): they want a new study. First ask, in one line, which products they have in mind, if any, by their sites ("Any products you want in the study? Their sites, or none."), naming the ones in `named` that are already kept. `price` 0 (this week's is free on their plan): then call `research_run` with what they named as `domains` (none named: no `domains`), with no question about the price. Otherwise put the price and the balance from `workspace` in the same question, and that it is charged only when at least 2 products can be read ("$0.88; your balance is $8.40. Run it, and any products you want in it?"); wait for a yes, then call `research_run` with their `domains`. `next` says which applies; follow it, and its wording rules. If it says the first AI visibility check comes first, say so and offer the audit routine. For an app, if `note` says the App Store searches are not checked yet, say the week's listing set comes first and offer it (the aso routine with `set`, whose search check is free); the study can be asked for once it is done. For an app the founder names apps by their App Store links ("Any apps you want in the study? Their App Store links, or none."). It runs a few minutes in the background: say so, and call `research` once more only when the founder comes back to it. Never call it again and again to wait. With an older study there, carry on with it below while the new one runs.
3. **The products**, one line each: the name and domain, its lens in plain words ("same job", "same buyers", "sells the same way"), "you named it" when `via` is "founder", and why it counts as a step ahead (`peers[].why`). An app: its name, its App Store link (`peers[].url`), its ratings, the searches it tops (`peers[].why`), and what was read in a few words (its price and in-app purchases, its screenshots, its last update, what its reviews say about money).
4. **The patterns**, the clearest first, one line each: early move or today, what they do or did, which of them with the page or launch (`evidence[].what` and `url`, with `source` and `at` for an early one, a quote only word for word as given), what the founder's site shows (`founder`), the first step (`step`), and the move with its price. "Acme and Beta each have a free invoice checker with no signup (acme.com/tools/check, beta.io/checker). Your site has none. First step: a VAT number checker for your invoice buyers. Move: a free tool on your site, $0.04." "Early move: when it started in 2023, Acme posted a Show HN (412 points, 180 comments)." An app: "Kodable and Tynker each offer a 7 day free trial on the yearly plan, and their reviews praise it (Kodable's reviews, Tynker's listing). Your listing shows one monthly plan and no trial. First step: a 7 day trial on a yearly plan, shown after the first level. Move: rework your paywall and trial, free." No pattern: name the products and say nothing two of them do is missing from the site (the listing, for an app). That is an answer, not a failure. Then the `results` link for the full read.
5. **Ask**, the way "Asking the founder" in SKILL.md says. In a question tool, one question, header "Copy which": each pattern as an option, its label the move in a few words, its description the evidence in a few words and the price, the one you would pick first with "(Recommended)"; then "One of these is not like me" while there is room (four options at most). Without a question tool, the same as one line each, numbered.
   - **Not like them** (other buyers, far bigger, not a product at all): ask which, with the products as options, and call `research_skip` with its domain (an app's key, or its App Store link). It is left out of this study's patterns now (a pattern it leaves with one product goes too) and never studied again. Call `research` again and show what is left. Said by mistake: `research_skip` with `undo: true`.
6. **A pattern picked** (here, or the move grow offered, its key the input): `grow_answer` with `yes` on its key first. Then read the routine its move's command names and run it from its first step, with the command's arguments. What the products do is that routine's starting point: the kind of free tool for build, what the first screen and signup do for convert, the board for launch, what the posts are about for notes. For an app it is the aso routine's part the command names (its `trial` part: what their paywall and trial do, and what their reviews say about it; `set`: the searches their name and subtitle carry). Everything else is that routine as usual.
   - **Record it as done.** Every `record_action` that routine makes for this work carries `meta.research` with the move's key, next to the meta the routine already sets. Where the routine records another way (notes, with `reach_mark`), call `record_action` once more with the same module, kind, title and url as that record (a post on X: COMMUNITIES, POST, the post's link) and `meta.research`: the same URL updates it. A pattern recorded this way is never offered again.
   - **A pattern no other routine covers** (its command is this routine with the key): say in one line what doing it takes, from the evidence. A change to the site: one PR (references/pr-conventions.md), `record_action` with `module` FIXES, `kind` PR, `meta.research`. Something the founder does by hand: say exactly what and where, end with "Tell me when it's done", and record it then.
   - **Already done** (the founder says they do it): `record_action` it right away with `meta.research` (SKILL.md, "Done is done"). Never ask about it again.
7. The hand-over (SKILL.md) once the move is finished.
