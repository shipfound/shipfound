# Routine: reach

Community replies and one-to-one inbox drafts. The founder posts and sends; Shipfound never does. Partly built; see the note.

Every hard line applies here more than anywhere: never post, never send, no karma farming, no bulk email, Gmail drafts only, at most 20 a day.

**What is not built yet:** thread discovery for Quora and Hacker News. Reddit's and X's run on Shipfound's server (`reach_find`), and the drafts land on Assisted posts in the results app: Reddit finds 5 threads a run plus a post where it fits, X finds up to 5 live conversations and writes a reply to each. For Quora and HN, this routine works on threads the founder brings, and on inbox drafts.

**Here, X is replies.** This routine joins conversations that are already happening. The founder's own posts about their product, on X and Reddit, are build in public notes and live in their own routine (notes.md); a boost lives in boost.md. Never turn Shipfound's own work into a post: no "today it wrote 2 pages" status reports, no numbers from Shipfound's own runs or visibility checks. A post like that tells the founder's followers nothing they can use.

**The people who asked for a feature** are told it shipped from the ideas routine (ideas.md, step 10): `ideas_replies_find` is this routine's run aimed at one feature that shipped, with the same search, checks, price and drafts. Those drafts land on Assisted posts with the rest, marked as a feature the founder shipped, and are read with `reach_drafts` and marked with `reach_mark` the same way.

Input: a thread URL, `signups`, `network`, or `cited`.

## Procedure

1. Call `workspace` with `repo` and `access`. Read the reddit, x and gmail areas.
2. **Reddit.** If the reddit area is red (no account), do no Reddit drafting. Otherwise tell the founder in two plain lines what a run does: it finds questions their buyers asked on Reddit this month and writes a reply for each, plus a post of their own where a subreddit allows it, for 8 credits, charged only when it finds something. It searches the product's own communities first (the one for the exact tool it is built for or plugs into, then the buyer's exact job), with one broad founder sub beside them, so the drafts are not all in r/SaaS and r/Entrepreneurs. Wait for a yes, then `reach_find` with platform reddit. Give them the link it returns: Assisted posts opens on the Reddit drafts, and they land there in about two minutes. Call `reach_drafts` once after that and say how many are ready. They post each one by hand from that page and mark it posted with the link. Never open or fetch Reddit yourself (SKILL.md, "Reddit: you post it"), and say once why the founder posts it by hand. End with "Tell me when you have posted them" and stop; the next move waits for that (step 7).
3. **X.** If the x area is red (no account signed in), do no X drafting. Otherwise tell the founder what a run does: it finds posts from the last couple of days where their buyers talk about the problem the product solves or ask what to use, and writes one reply for each, for 8 credits, charged only when it finds something. Wait for a yes, then `reach_find` with platform x and give them the link: Assisted posts opens on the X drafts. Call `reach_drafts` once after about two minutes and say how many are ready, or why it found none. Each draft opens X's reply box with the reply filled in; they post it and mark it posted with the link. End with "Tell me when you have posted them" and stop. Ask about the next move only after they say they are done (step 7).
   Run only the platform the founder asked about. Asked for "replies" with no platform, ask which one first. Never start a second run to get different drafts.
4. **A thread URL.** Reddit: call `reach_drafts` with platform reddit first. A subreddit in its `closed` list gets no reply: say its line and stop (a banned one, never from another account either). Ask the founder to paste the question and the sub's rules from the thread, draft one reply that answers it, with no link, and give it to them to copy, with the thread link to open and paste it into. Name the product only when `mentionsLeft` is above 0 and the sub's rules allow a founder to say it; otherwise the reply helps and stops, with no "I built" either, and say why in one line. X, Quora, Hacker News: open it in the browser, read the thread and the community's rules, draft one reply (Quora and HN carry no link; X may carry one), fill the reply box and stop. Either way the founder edits and posts. When they give you the posted URL, call `record_action` with `module` COMMUNITIES, `kind` POST.
   Drafts from a run: when the founder says which they posted or skipped and has not marked them on the page, call `reach_mark` for each (ids from `reach_drafts`), with the link to their reply when they have it. A draft left unmarked stays waiting and is offered again.
5. **signups**: with a Gmail connector, find new-signup notification emails from the last 7 days, leave out anyone already written to (search Gmail for `to:<address>` in Sent and Drafts first), and draft one personal note per signup left (from the founder, plain, one question about what they are trying to do). At most 20 drafts today, counting any made earlier today. Call `record_action` with `module` INBOX, `kind` DRAFT for the batch, `url` https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#drafts, `meta: { count }`.
6. **network**: propose up to 20 people from the founder's sent mail who would plausibly care, with one line on why each. The founder approves names before any draft exists. Then draft as in step 5.
7. **cited**: the "best X" and "X alternatives" lists AI answers cite for the founder's buyers, without them on it. Call `outreach_targets` (4 credits for a batch with new pages to look up, free when every page was looked up before; say so in the same line). Skip, with `skip`, every page whose site sells a product like the founder's: its own list of its rivals will not add them; say which in one line. For the rest, one at a time: open the page in the browser, then draft one short personal note from the founder to the `author` at `email`: what their list covers, the one line on why this product belongs on it, a link. No attachments, no follow-up sequence, nothing that reads like a template, at most 20 drafts today counting any made earlier. With no `email`, find the site's contact page or form and fill it for the founder to send. After each, `record_action` with `module` INBOX, `kind` DRAFT, `title` "Asked to join <the list's title>", `url` the list page itself (one action per page), `meta: { type: "outreach", url: <the same page url> }`. Never write to a page the founder skipped, and never ask twice.
8. Without a Gmail connector that can create drafts, say so and skip inbox work. Without browser tools, give the founder the draft text to paste. Then the hand-over (SKILL.md), once the founder says they have posted or sent what this routine handed them: offer the next move.

Never draft from an account the founder does not own, never ask anyone to upvote, never write a review or testimonial.
